J.R.R. Tolkien’s hobbits are the unassuming heroes of Middle-earth, creatures of comfort and quiet routine. Residing in the lush, sheltered land of the Shire, they are a rustic and peace-loving people, fond of simple pleasures: good food, fine ale, cheerful company, and the familiarity of their own hearths.Typically smaller than Men, hobbits are characterized by their furry feet, affable faces, and a remarkable ability to move with stealth.
